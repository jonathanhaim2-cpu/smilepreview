## Invisalign: The Gold Standard

Invisalign is the most established, most widely studied, and most comprehensive clear aligner system in the world. But it's also the most expensive.

### What Makes Invisalign Unique

**SmartTrack® aligner material** is engineered to apply a consistent, gentle force throughout each tray's wear period — more predictably than generic plastics.

**Attachments** — small tooth-colored composite bumps — are bonded to specific teeth to help aligners exert complex forces for rotations and vertical movements.

**In-office expertise** means your orthodontist can catch problems early, make adjustments to the digital plan, and order refinements before your case goes sideways.

**Widest case range:** Invisalign can address moderate crowding, Class II/III malocclusions, open bites, crossbites, and more complex bite issues that at-home brands cannot.

### Invisalign Best For

✓ Moderate-to-severe alignment issues  
✓ Patients with bite problems (overbite, underbite, crossbite)  
✓ Teens and growing patients  
✓ Patients who want maximum predictability  
✓ Anyone with complex tooth movements

### Invisalign Not Ideal For

✗ Budget-constrained patients with mild issues  
✗ Patients who want to avoid office visits  
✗ Very mild cases (overkill in complexity and cost)

### Invisalign Pricing

- **Lite** (minor issues, up to 14 trays): $1,500–$3,000
- **Moderate** (up to 26 trays): $3,000–$5,500
- **Comprehensive** (unlimited trays): $4,500–$8,000

## Byte: The Speed Champion

Byte launched in 2018 and quickly became one of the most popular at-home aligner brands by combining a competitive price with an innovative acceleration technology — the HyperByte®.

### What Makes Byte Unique

**HyperByte® vibration device** uses high-frequency vibration (HFV) to improve aligner seating and potentially accelerate bone remodeling. Included free with every treatment (a $400 value).

**Fastest average treatment:** 3–5 months average completion for mild cases — significantly faster than other at-home brands.

**For-life guarantee:** If your teeth shift after treatment, Byte provides new aligners at no additional charge — one of the strongest warranties in the industry.

**Night-only option (Byte-at-Night):** Wear aligners 10 hours at night only. Takes 5–6 months but suits people who hate wearing aligners during the day.

**All-inclusive pricing:** $1,895 covers everything — aligners, HyperByte, retainers, whitening, and the for-life guarantee. No surprise fees.

### Byte Best For

✓ Mild crowding or spacing  
✓ Patients prioritizing speed  
✓ Adults who want at-home treatment  
✓ People comfortable with self-managed treatment  
✓ Night-time-only preference

### Byte Not Ideal For

✗ Moderate to severe crowding  
✗ Significant bite issues (overbite, underbite)  
✗ Patients who prefer in-person care  
✗ Anyone whose case requires attachments

### Byte Pricing

- **Byte All-Day:** $1,895 flat
- **Byte At-Night:** $2,295 flat
- Financing available: as low as $69/month with Affirm

## Candid: The Supervised Middle Ground

Candid sits between the fully at-home brands (Byte, SmileDirectClub) and full in-office treatment (Invisalign). Its key differentiator: **every patient is monitored by a licensed orthodontist throughout their entire treatment**, not just at the initial scan.

### What Makes Candid Unique

**CandidMonitoring™:** You use a CandidSensor device to submit weekly progress scans. A board-certified orthodontist reviews every scan and can flag issues before they become problems. This level of ongoing oversight is unavailable from any other remote brand.

**Orthodontist-only network:** Every Candid patient is supervised by a board-certified orthodontist — not a general dentist or algorithm.

**Candid Studios:** Physical locations in major cities for professional 3D scanning (no messy at-home impressions). Currently in 35+ cities.

**Refined case selection:** Candid only accepts cases they're confident they can treat well. If your case isn't suitable, they'll tell you upfront rather than take your money.

### Candid Best For

✓ Mild to moderate alignment issues  
✓ Safety-conscious patients  
✓ People who want professional oversight without in-office visits  
✓ Patients in cities with Candid Studios  
✓ Those who've been turned down by Byte/SmileDirectClub for case complexity

### Candid Not Ideal For

✗ Very mild cases (Byte is faster and cheaper)  
✗ Complex cases requiring in-person orthodontist  
✗ Patients not near a Candid Studio  
✗ Those needing attachments for complex movements

### Candid Pricing

- $2,400–$3,200 depending on case complexity
- Monthly financing available from approximately $89/month
- FSA/HSA eligible

## Head-to-Head Comparison: The Key Differences

### 1. Doctor Oversight: Candid Wins for At-Home

Invisalign has the highest oversight (in-person, every 6–8 weeks). For at-home options, Candid's model is significantly safer than Byte's — your orthodontist reviews progress weekly, not just initially.

Byte has only a one-time dentist review before treatment begins. If something goes wrong during treatment, you're largely on your own until you reach out to support.

### 2. Speed: Byte Wins

Average treatment times:
- Byte: 3–5 months (average 3 months for simple cases)
- Candid: 6–12 months
- Invisalign: 6–18 months

If speed matters, Byte is the clear leader — though only for mild cases.

### 3. Case Range: Invisalign Wins

| Case Type | Invisalign | Candid | Byte |
|-----------|-----------|--------|------|
| Mild crowding | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Moderate crowding | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Severe crowding | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Minor overbite | ✓ | Limited | ✗ |
| Significant overbite | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Crossbite | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Open bite | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |

### 4. Cost: Byte Wins (for mild cases)

For the same mild crowding case:
- Byte: $1,895 all-in
- Candid: ~$2,400
- Invisalign: $3,000–$5,000

The cost gap is significant, but you're getting meaningfully different levels of care.

### 5. After-Treatment Warranty: Byte Wins

Byte's For-Life Guarantee provides free replacement aligners if teeth shift after treatment — potentially saving thousands long-term. Candid offers 6-month touch-up coverage. Invisalign typically doesn't include a formal relapse warranty.

## Which Should You Choose?

### Go with Invisalign if:
Your case is moderate to complex, you have bite issues, you want the most proven treatment, or you simply want the best possible outcome and the peace of mind of in-person oversight.

### Go with Byte if:
Your case is mild (slight crowding, minor spacing), you're confident your teeth are healthy, and you want the fastest results at the lowest price. The HyperByte is a genuine differentiator.

### Go with Candid if:
You have a mild to moderate case and want professional orthodontist monitoring throughout treatment — without paying full in-office prices. The right choice for safety-conscious patients who aren't near a Byte-compatible orthodontist.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I switch from Byte to Invisalign mid-treatment?**  
A: Yes, but it requires starting over with new scans and a new treatment plan. You'll pay full cost for Invisalign. Avoid this by choosing correctly from the start.

**Q: Is Invisalign worth the extra cost over Byte?**  
A: For mild cases, no — the outcomes are comparable. For moderate or complex cases, yes — Invisalign handles complexity that Byte simply cannot address.

**Q: Are all three FDA-cleared?**  
A: Yes. Invisalign, Byte, and Candid all use FDA-cleared Class II medical devices. The difference is clinical oversight, not regulatory status.

**Q: Does Byte require in-person visits?**  
A: No — Byte is entirely at-home. You order an impression kit, make your molds, ship them back, and receive your aligners by mail. No office visits required (unless you use a BestSmile partner location for scanning).

**Q: Which has the best customer service?**  
A: Based on customer reviews, Candid and Byte rank higher than SmileDirectClub. Invisalign satisfaction depends heavily on your individual orthodontist.
