## What "Before and After" Actually Means in Orthodontics

A successful clear aligner result isn't just about aesthetics. Orthodontists evaluate outcomes across several dimensions:

- **Alignment** — are teeth straight and properly positioned?
- **Spacing** — have gaps been closed or crowding resolved?
- **Bite** — does the upper jaw properly close over the lower?
- **Midline** — do upper and lower dental midlines align?
- **Overjet and overbite** — are these within normal range?

The before-and-after photos you see in ads typically show the most dramatic cases. Real-world results for average patients are meaningful but more subtle.

**Treatment duration:** 4 months

## What Factors Affect Your Before-and-After Result?

### 1. Case Complexity
The most predictive factor. Mild cases achieve excellent results with any brand. Complex cases require expert clinical judgment.

### 2. Compliance
Wearing aligners 20–22 hours per day is non-negotiable. Studies show compliance is the #1 predictor of treatment success. Every hour skipped slows tooth movement.

### 3. Aligner Brand and Quality
Thin, poorly manufactured aligners don't move teeth as predictably. Premium brands (Invisalign, Candid) use medical-grade materials with strict quality controls.

### 4. Provider Skill (Invisalign)
For complex cases, your orthodontist's experience designing your ClinCheck treatment plan dramatically affects the outcome. Seek a Diamond or Platinum Invisalign provider for complex cases.

### 5. Biology
Tooth movement speed varies between individuals. Age, bone density, genetics, and overall health all play a role. This is why treatment estimates are always ranges.

### 6. Retention
Maintaining your results long-term requires diligent retainer wear. Teeth will relapse without retention — it's not optional.

## Realistic Expectations: What Clear Aligners Can and Cannot Do

### Clear aligners CAN:
- Correct mild to moderate crowding
- Close spaces and gaps
- Improve overall arch alignment
- Correct mild overbite, underbite, and open bite (Invisalign)
- Rotate teeth (with attachments via Invisalign)
- Improve smile aesthetics dramatically

### Clear aligners CANNOT:
- Fix severe skeletal discrepancies (jaw surgery may be needed)
- Move teeth that haven't erupted
- Correct severe Class III malocclusion (significant underbite)
- Replace lost teeth
- Fix jaw bone defects
- Work if worn inconsistently

## After Treatment: What to Expect

The finish line isn't "last tray day." Here's what comes next:

### Retainer phase (critical)
- First 3–6 months: wear retainers full-time (22+ hours/day)
- After 6 months: nightly wear indefinitely

Skip retainers and your teeth WILL shift back. This is biological reality, not a scare tactic.

### Whitening opportunity
With clean, well-aligned teeth, whitening results are dramatically better. Many patients whiten immediately after completing treatment.

### Maintenance
Regular dental cleanings every 6 months help maintain your results long-term. Aligned teeth accumulate less plaque in hard-to-reach spots.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long do clear aligner results last?**  
A: Results are permanent as long as you wear retainers. Teeth will gradually shift without retention — this is true for all orthodontic treatment including braces.

**Q: Can I see my results before starting treatment?**  
A: With Invisalign, yes — your orthodontist creates a ClinCheck 3D simulation showing your projected final result. At-home brands don't typically offer this.

**Q: What if my results aren't perfect after finishing?**  
A: Most brands include refinements — additional aligners to address any gaps between predicted and actual tooth positions. Always clarify the refinement policy before starting.

**Q: Do teeth look different immediately after removing aligners?**  
A: Your teeth may feel slightly sensitive after completing treatment, but results are visible immediately. Some patients experience minor tooth indentations from attachments that polish out over 1–2 weeks.
